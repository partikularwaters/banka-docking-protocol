# Banka Handoff

Use this only when the assessment status is `Ready for Banka` and the owner has
chosen to continue. Package:

- the completed `ADOPTION-ASSESSMENT.md`;
- the accepted scope source, regardless of filename or origin;
- verified commands, repository facts, and environment-specific evidence;
- unresolved stabilization backlog items with owners and priorities;
- explicit exclusions, prohibited actions, authority limits, and sensitive-data
  handling rules; and
- unresolved external dependencies, their owners, and the next responsible
  action.

Point the receiver to the current
[Banka repository](https://github.com/partikularwaters/Banka) and its
[authoritative protocol](https://github.com/partikularwaters/Banka/blob/main/protocol/Banka.md).
Banka independently performs its own scoping confirmation, tier decision,
project-state generation, skill discovery, and runtime guidance.

Do not prescribe a tier, create Banka-managed files, copy Banka templates or
skills, claim adoption is complete, or infer authority to deploy or mutate
production. After delivering the package, record `Handed off` and stop unless
the owner separately authorizes the downstream workflow.
