# Optional Scope-Clarification Handoff

Use this handoff only after the assessment finds scope materially unclear and
the owner chooses a scope-clarification route. ASMP is optional; another adequate
workflow or owner-supplied scope artifact is equally acceptable.

Package only:

- known purpose and intended outcome;
- stakeholders, beneficiaries, and decision owners;
- current project reality and environment distinctions;
- constraints, commitments, prohibited actions, and sensitive-data boundaries;
- named evidence with authority and confidence limits;
- open scope questions and why they block readiness judgment; and
- the expected scope result and owner of the next decision.

If selected, use the current
[Adaptive Scope Mapping Protocol](https://github.com/partikularwaters/adaptive-scope-mapping-protocol).
Do not copy its interview logic, modes, output schema, templates, or adapters
into this repository. Stop this protocol while clarification runs. Resume only
when the owner supplies an adequate result; accept it regardless of filename or
provenance.

Do not pass credentials, private logs, customer data, or unsupported production
claims. Link to controlled evidence or provide a redacted description.
